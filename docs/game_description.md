# Retro Game Summary: Jetpack

The name **Jetpack** (or **Jetpac**) refers to two legendary retro arcade-platformer classics that defined distinct eras of PC and home computer gaming. Below is a comprehensive overview of both titles, their mechanics, objectives, and legacy.

---

## 1. Jetpack (1993, MS-DOS)
**Developer:** Adam Pedersen  
**Publisher:** Software Creations / Epic MegaGames  
**Genre:** Action / Puzzle-Platformer  

### Overview & Objective
In *Jetpack* (1993), you play as a space explorer equipped with a high-powered jetpack and a Phase Shifter tool. The primary objective in each single-screen or scrolling level is to **collect all the green emeralds** scattered across the maze. Once every emerald has been collected, the level's exit portal opens, allowing you to proceed to the next stage.

### Core Mechanics
* **Flight & Fuel Management:** Flying with your jetpack allows free vertical and horizontal movement. However, the jetpack consumes fuel rapidly while thrusting. Players must navigate using ladders, vines, and platforms to conserve fuel, while picking up fuel canisters or landing on refueling grids to replenish energy.
* **Phase Shifter (Digging/Tunneling):** A key tool inspired by *Lode Runner*, the Phase Shifter lets players temporarily disintegrate specific types of brick blocks. This creates temporary paths or tunnels to access enclosed emeralds or bypass obstacles.
* **Movement & Controls:**
  * **Arrow Keys / Joystick:** Walk left/right, climb ladders/vines, duck.
  * **Up Arrow / Thrust Key:** Fire the jetpack to fly upwards.
  * **Spacebar / Phase Key:** Fire the Phase Shifter to dissolve phaseable bricks.

### Environmental Elements & Hazards
* **Terrain & Blocks:** Standard Bricks, Phaseable Bricks, Ice Blocks (slippery), Conveyor Belts, Steel Pillars, Ladders, and Teleporters.
* **Collectibles:** Green Emeralds (level objective), Gold & Gems (bonus points), and Single/Double Fuel Canisters.
* **Enemies & Hazards:** 
  * **Flitzers & Homing Missiles:** Enemies that follow fixed paths or actively track the player.
  * **Spikes & Energy Drains:** Static hazards that destroy the player on contact or rapidly strip away jetpack fuel.
  * **Turrets & Cannons:** Fire projectiles at timed intervals.

### Standout Feature: Built-In Level Editor
One of *Jetpack's* most celebrated features was its included, user-friendly **Level Editor**. Players could build custom maps from scratch, place all enemy types, terrain, and emeralds, and save/share custom level packs. This built a passionate modding community that created thousands of custom levels.

### Multiplayer
* Supports up to **8 players** in local hotseat (turn-based) multiplayer mode.

## 2. Jetpac (1983, ZX Spectrum / Commodore 64 / BBC Micro)
**Developer / Publisher:** Ultimate Play the Game (Tim & Chris Stamper, later Rare)  
**Genre:** Fixed-Screen Arcade Shooter / Platformer  

### Overview & Objective
In the original 1983 *Jetpac*, you control **Jetman**, an astronaut stranded on alien planets. The primary goal is to **assemble your rocket ship**, fill it with fuel, enter the capsule, and blast off to the next planet while surviving hostile alien creatures.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Assemble the Rocket (Stages 1, 5, 9...):** Collect three rocket segments dropped from space and stack them onto the base landing pad.
2. **Refuel the Ship:** Collect 6 fuel pods one at a time and drop them into the assembled rocket.
3. **Takeoff:** Fly into the fueled rocket capsule to launch to the next world.
4. **Survive Alien Waves:** Each planet features distinct alien species (flying saucers, meteorites, space monsters) that attack in continuous waves.
5. **Score Points:** Destroy aliens using your continuous Quad Photon Laser Phasers and collect falling bonus items (gold bars, diamonds, isotopes, slush puppies).

### Key Features & Mechanics
* **Hydrovac Jet Pac:** Provides unlimited flight thrust with momentum physics.
* **Screen Wrapping:** Moving off the left edge of the screen teleports Jetman to the right edge (and vice-versa).
* **Reset Cycle:** Every 4th level, Jetman lands on a new planet requiring a brand-new rocket assembly (which also rewards an extra life).

## Summary Comparison

| Feature | *Jetpack* (1993 MS-DOS) | *Jetpac* (1983 ZX Spectrum) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Collect all green emeralds & reach exit portal | Assemble & refuel rocket ship to blast off |
| **Special Tool** | Phase Shifter (dissolves blocks) | Quad Photon Laser (shoots enemies) |
| **Fuel System** | Limited fuel (must collect canisters/grids) | Fuel used to fill rocket ship (unlimited jetpack thrust) |
| **Level Structure** | Puzzle-like mazes with ladders, ice & teleporters | Open single-screen platforms with screen wrapping |
| **Level Editor** | Included full graphical map editor | None |
| **Legacy** | Epic MegaGames Shareware classic | Established Ultimate Play the Game / Rare history |
