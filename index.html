HOW TO PLAY & CONTROLS

🎮 CONTROLS

  • ← → or A D : Walk Left / Right
  • ↑ ↓ or W S : Climb Ladders & Vines
  • SHIFT or UP : Fire Jetpack (Consumes Fuel)
  • Z or SPACE : Fire Phase Shifter Beam
  • K or BACKSPACE : Self-Destruct / Respawn
  • P or ESC : Pause Game

  • Phase Shifter ⚡: Temporarily dissolves phaseable brick walls to create tunnels. Blocks rebuild after a delay!
  • Teleporters 🔮: Walk or land on any Teleporter Pad to instantly warp across linked nodes.
